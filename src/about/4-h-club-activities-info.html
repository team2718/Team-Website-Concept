4-H Club Activities

Our 4-H Club is open to students of all ages. You can find 4-H-specific information on the Oklahoma County 4-H site.

  • 7th-12th grade participate on a FRC or FRC team and/or the STEAM Volunteer Squad.
  • 1st-6th grade participate in fun robotics activities (if not in robot season – FIRST Mondays are Dec-May!) and/or on a competition team.
  • Full team meetings occur about 6 times each year, but a variety of other camps an activities are offered through Team OKC and Oklahoma County 4-H as well.
  • Cost to join 4-H is $20 per year for 3rd grade and up. 1st-2nd grade may participate as Cloverbuds for no cost.
